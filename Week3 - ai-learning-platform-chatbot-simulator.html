🎓 Learning Concepts

🧠 What is Machine Learning?

Instead of programming every possible response, we give the AI examples and it learns patterns. Just like how you learned to recognize cats by seeing many cat pictures!

📊 Training Data

The question-answer pairs you create are "training data". Real AI models are trained on millions or billions of examples to become really smart!

🎯 Pattern Matching

Your chatbot looks for patterns in your question and matches it to what it learned. Different matching methods work differently:

  • Exact Match: Must match perfectly (like typing a password)
  • Keyword Match: Looks for matching words (like search engines)
  • Smart Match: Understands similar meanings (like real AI)

🌡️ Temperature/Creativity

Temperature controls randomness in AI responses:

  • Low (0.0-0.3): Same answer every time, very predictable
  • Medium (0.4-0.6): Balanced, slight variations
  • High (0.7-1.0): Creative, different answers each time
